Be Bright, Cover Light

We are a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to raising awareness for Skin cancer and promoting sun safety. Through education, fundraising, and donations we support the Skin Cancer Foundation and want to provide sun-protective supplies to the Shade foundation.

Our Mission

Sun Safe Society is a student-led non-profit dedicated to combating skin cancer and UV-related health risks through comprehensive education, community outreach, and accessible sun safety resources. We empower students and families with knowledge about sun protection, early skin cancer detection, and healthy habits to enjoy the sun responsibly.
